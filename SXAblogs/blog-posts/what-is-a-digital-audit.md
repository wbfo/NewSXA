## What a Digital Audit Actually Is

A digital audit is a structured diagnostic of your entire digital infrastructure — not just your website, but every system, platform, and signal that determines whether potential customers find you, trust you, and contact you.

It is not a website review. It is not an SEO report. It is not a social media analysis. It covers all of those, but it also examines the layers most agencies never look at: AI visibility, directory consistency, schema markup, intake systems, and revenue leak analysis.

The goal is not to tell you your website looks good or bad. The goal is to show you exactly what your business looks like to a customer who has never heard of you and is currently searching for what you offer.

---

FINDING 01

### Website and technical infrastructure

A digital audit examines whether your website is structured so that search engines and AI systems can read and trust it. This includes page speed, mobile performance, metadata, and schema markup — the structured data layer that tells AI engines who you are, where you are, and what you do. Businesses implementing LocalBusiness schema see 20–30% higher click-through rates than those without it. Most websites pass a visual inspection and fail a technical one.

FINDING 02

### Brand identity and NAP consistency

Your business name, address, and phone number must appear identically across every platform where your business exists. Only 30% of small businesses have fully consistent NAP data across major directories. A single inconsistency — a street abbreviation that differs between your website and Yelp, a phone number updated two years ago and never corrected on three directories — signals unreliability to both AI systems and search engines. NAP consistency improves local rankings by an average of 23%.

FINDING 03

### Local SEO and AI visibility

An audit checks whether your business appears in local search results and, increasingly, whether it appears in AI-generated recommendations on ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overview. These are now separate tests. Only 12% of AI citations come from Google's top-10 results. A business can rank on the first page of Google and be completely absent from every AI response — invisible to the 31% of the US population now using generative AI search.

FINDING 04

### Social media infrastructure

Not just whether you have accounts, but whether those accounts are active, consistently branded, linked correctly, and feeding into your overall digital authority. Algorithms currently bury 95% of organic business content. A broken Instagram link on your website is a trust gap. An account with thousands of followers not connected to your business profile is dead equity.

FINDING 05

### Systems, intake, and revenue leaks

This is the section most agencies skip entirely. An audit examines whether your business is capturing the leads your visibility generates. 62% of calls to small service businesses go unanswered, costing the average business $126,000 per year. A business that achieves visibility and then loses the lead at the phone has solved half the problem.

FINDING 06

### AI readiness assessment

A complete digital audit now includes an evaluation of whether your business is positioned for AI-driven discovery and whether automation — specifically AI voice agents — could close the gap between leads generated and leads captured. Businesses using AI voice agents report a 300–800% ROI in year one.
