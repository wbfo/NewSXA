A healthcare staffing company in Nashville engaged RW Ads for a Strategy First Engagement. In 45 days, they received a clear picture of their ideal client profile, a mapped buyer journey, and a step-by-step marketing roadmap aligned with their revenue goals. The work was sound. The strategy was well-defined.

When they began executing against the roadmap — content publishing, lead generation campaigns, local visibility efforts — results came in below the strategy's projections. A digital audit commissioned three months into execution identified what the strategy had been built on top of without checking: no schema markup on any page, GBP showing a category mismatch from a pre-pivot service model, absent from ChatGPT and Perplexity for every search their buyer persona was using, and NAP inconsistent across nine directories that Google's local algorithm used to score entity confidence.

The strategy was right. The infrastructure it was being applied to was unverified.

## What RW Ads Offers

RW Ads is a U.S.-based marketing strategy agency positioning itself as a Fractional CMO partner. Their core offerings include a Strategy First Engagement — a 45-day engagement to define the ideal customer, map the buyer journey, and create a step-by-step marketing roadmap; Strategic Marketing Retainer Packages — Fractional CMO-level oversight of marketing execution; a Total Online Presence Audit — a data-backed view of performance across SEO, social, and local search; and Brand Builder — brand identity development for startups or rebrands.

RW Ads describes their approach as "strategy-first" and focuses on aligning marketing efforts with business goals through leadership, direction, and accountability. Their Total Online Presence Audit is positioned as a focused introduction to their Strategy-First approach — identifying where a business stands across channels.

What RW Ads's approach is designed to answer: where should marketing investment be directed, and is execution aligned with business goals? What it is not designed to answer: whether the AI infrastructure beneath the marketing strategy is functioning — specifically, schema configuration for generative AI, AI platform citation rates across ChatGPT and Perplexity, NAP consistency across 30+ directories, and GBP entity accuracy that allows AI systems to confidently cite the business.

---

FINDING 01 — STRATEGY PRESUPPOSES THE INFRASTRUCTURE IS READY TO SUPPORT IT

The commercial insurance broker scenario from the digital audit vs marketing audit post applies directly here. A marketing strategy — however well-defined — produces results in proportion to the quality of the infrastructure it is applied to. If a business's GBP is showing the wrong service category, its schema markup is absent, and it is invisible in the AI platforms where its buyer persona is making decisions, the strategy will underperform relative to its design. This is not a failure of strategy. It is a consequence of strategy running ahead of infrastructure verification. RW Ads's Strategy First Engagement defines the roadmap. An SX Audit verifies whether the infrastructure is ready to support what the roadmap recommends.

FINDING 02 — RW ADS'S ONLINE PRESENCE AUDIT AND AN SX AUDIT ASSESS DIFFERENT DIMENSIONS

RW Ads offers a Total Online Presence Audit positioned around performance across SEO, social, and local search — a channel-performance assessment. SX Audits' AI Readiness Audit specifically tests the five dimensions that determine AI citation and AI-driven discovery: AI platform visibility testing (manual query runs across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews), schema markup and entity configuration, GBP status and accuracy, NAP consistency across 30+ directories, and third-party citation quality. These are different assessments with different scopes. A channel performance review that identifies underperforming organic search does not diagnose why AI platforms are not citing the business. The diagnosis layer is different.

FINDING 03 — FRACTIONAL CMO STRATEGY DOES NOT INCLUDE GEO IMPLEMENTATION

Generative Engine Optimization — the discipline of configuring digital infrastructure for AI citation — is outside the scope of most marketing strategy engagements. Fewer than 12% of marketing strategy teams have a defined GEO approach. A Fractional CMO engagement defines which channels to invest in, what messaging to use, and how to align campaigns with buyer journey stages. It does not implement the schema markup that allows AI systems to confidently identify and recommend the business, verify NAP consistency across 30+ directories, or test AI platform citation rates. These are infrastructure-layer technical implementations — prerequisites to the strategy layer.

FINDING 04 — THE CORRECT SEQUENCE IS: INFRASTRUCTURE DIAGNOSIS, THEN STRATEGY ENGAGEMENT

For a business investing in marketing strategy — whether through RW Ads or any fractional CMO engagement — the correct sequence is: commission a full digital and AI readiness audit to establish what is working and what is broken in the infrastructure; implement the audit's priority fixes; then commission the strategy engagement to build on the corrected foundation. This is not an argument against RW Ads's approach — their strategy methodology is sound and their focus on measurable growth is legitimate. It is an argument about sequence: the audit identifies whether the infrastructure is ready for the strategy to perform at the level the strategy is designed to deliver.

FINDING FIVE — BOTH SERVICES SERVE DIFFERENT STAGES OF THE SAME OBJECTIVE

A business that has never had a digital audit and is considering a strategy engagement needs the audit first. A business that has completed a digital audit, corrected its infrastructure, and now needs strategic direction for its marketing investment benefits from a fractional CMO engagement. The services are not alternatives to each other — they are sequential. SX Audits is not a marketing strategy service. RW Ads is not a technical AI infrastructure auditor. A business that uses both in the right order gets the full picture: infrastructure verified and optimized, strategy built on a foundation capable of supporting it.
