# Med Spa Local SEO Audit: The 12-Point Checklist Patients Actually Care About

---

A med spa on the Upper East Side was running $8,000 a month in Instagram ads. New patient bookings were flat. The owner assumed the problem was creative — better photos, different angles, a different offer. She was about to increase the budget.

An audit told a different story. The Google Business Profile listed the wrong zip code. The Healthgrades listing had an address that didn't match the website. When someone searched "botox near me" or "med spa Upper East Side" in ChatGPT, three competitors appeared. Her business did not.

The ads were spending money to send traffic to a funnel that was already leaking from multiple points. The fix wasn't more spend. It was infrastructure.

---

## Why Med Spa Local SEO Is Different

Med spa patients make high-trust, high-consideration decisions. Thirty-three percent have household incomes exceeding $100,000 annually. They research thoroughly — 93% of patients search online before booking a first appointment. They read reviews carefully, check credentials, compare multiple providers, and frequently ask AI platforms for recommendations before taking any other action.

Patient acquisition costs for med spas have doubled since 2023. In this environment, a leaking funnel is not a minor problem — it is the primary growth constraint.

---

## Finding 01: GBP COMPLETENESS

Your Google Business Profile needs to be fully optimized for healthcare aesthetics. This means: correct primary category (most med spas should be "Medical Spa"), accurate address and phone, complete service list with individual services listed (not just "med spa services"), hours updated for every holiday, and photos that include the facility, staff, and treatment areas. The local 3-pack captures 44% of all clicks from high-intent local searches. Med spas in position one for "med spa near me" capture 40–60% of all clicks for that search.

## Finding 02: HEALTHGRADES AND SPECIALTY DIRECTORIES

Med spa patients use Healthgrades, ZocDoc, RealSelf, and Yelp as verification tools before booking. Each platform needs to be claimed, complete, and consistent with your GBP. Inconsistent NAP data across these platforms suppresses both local search rankings and AI recommendation rates. AI systems cross-reference these platforms when forming recommendations — a med spa missing from RealSelf is missing from a significant portion of its AI citation signal in the aesthetics category.

## Finding 03: AI VISIBILITY FOR AESTHETIC SEARCHES

Run this test: open ChatGPT and search "best botox in [city]," "top med spa for [your primary treatment] near [neighborhood]," and "who do you recommend for [your top service] in [city]." Document your appearance rate. The med spas appearing consistently in these responses have schema markup, complete directory listings, and original citable content about their specific treatments and credentials. Most don't appear because their digital infrastructure doesn't give AI enough structured signal.

## Finding 04: REVIEW VOLUME AND RECENCY

Med spa patients read more reviews than almost any other service category before booking. They read an average of 10+ reviews and spend nearly 14 minutes doing so. A med spa with 45 Google reviews and a last review posted six weeks ago is at a significant disadvantage against a competitor with 180 reviews and a review from three days ago. Review generation needs to be a continuous system, not a periodic push.

## Finding 05: TREATMENT-SPECIFIC CONTENT

Each of your primary services — botox, filler, laser treatments, body contouring, skin care — needs its own dedicated, structured page that answers patient questions directly. "What is the recovery time after botox?" "How long do results last?" "Who is a good candidate for [treatment]?" These pages serve dual purposes: they answer SEO queries and they provide citation targets for AI systems that are increasingly asked these questions directly.

## Finding 06: INTAKE AND AFTER-HOURS CAPTURE

Med spa booking decisions frequently happen after 5 PM when patients are researching at home. If your contact form produces no response until the next morning, and your phone goes to a generic voicemail, you are losing patients to competitors who have real-time booking available. 62% of small business calls go unanswered. In a category where a single new patient can be worth $2,000–$5,000 in lifetime revenue, after-hours lead capture is not optional.

---

## The 12-Point Checklist

1. GBP claimed, verified, and fully complete including all services
2. Primary GBP category set to "Medical Spa" or most specific applicable category
3. NAP identical across website, GBP, Yelp, Healthgrades, and RealSelf
4. LocalBusiness and MedicalBusiness schema implemented on website
5. Treatment-specific landing pages with FAQ structure for top three services
6. AI visibility tested: appears in ChatGPT and Perplexity for primary service queries
7. Review count and recency competitive with top local competitors
8. Review response rate: all reviews responded to within 72 hours
9. Website mobile load time under three seconds
10. After-hours inquiry capture: contact form, booking system, or AI voice agent
11. Google Analytics or equivalent confirming traffic sources and conversion tracking
12. Schema validated using Google's Rich Results Test with zero critical errors
