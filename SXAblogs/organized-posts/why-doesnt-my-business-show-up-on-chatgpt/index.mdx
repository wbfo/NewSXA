When someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI 
"who is the best [your service] in [your city]" — 
what happens?

For most businesses, nothing. They don't appear.
Not because they're bad at what they do.
Because their digital infrastructure gives AI systems 
nothing to work with.

## What AI Search Actually Looks For

AI engines don't return ten blue links.
They return one confident answer.

To give that answer, they need to verify a business 
exists, is legitimate, and is relevant to the query.
They do this by checking:

- Is there an owned website with structured data markup?
- Is the Google Business Profile claimed and consistent?
- Does the business name appear the same way across all platforms?
- Is there original content the AI can cite as a source?
- Do other authoritative sources mention this business?

If any of these are missing — and for most local 
businesses, most of them are — the AI skips the 
business entirely and recommends a competitor 
who has them.

## The Five Reasons Businesses Are Invisible to AI

<Finding label="FINDING 01" title="No owned website or schema markup">
AI agents need a structured data source to cite.
A website with LocalBusiness schema tells AI exactly
what the business does, where it operates, and how
to contact it. Without schema, the AI has to guess —
and it defaults to businesses that have it.
</Finding>

<Finding label="FINDING 02" title="Unclaimed or inconsistent Google Business Profile">
Google AI Overviews pull heavily from GBP data.
An unclaimed profile means the AI can't verify 
the business is active. Inconsistent NAP data 
(different name or phone number across platforms) 
signals low confidence. Low confidence = no mention.
</Finding>

<Finding label="FINDING 03" title="Name confusion in search">
If a business name matches multiple unrelated 
companies, AI systems cannot confidently attribute 
information to the right one. The result: the AI 
ignores all of them and recommends a business with 
an unambiguous identity.
</Finding>

<Finding label="FINDING 04" title="No original content to cite">
AI systems preferentially cite content that directly 
answers the question being asked. A business with 
no blog, no FAQ page, and no original written content 
gives the AI nothing to quote. A competitor with 
three well-structured blog posts answering the same 
question gets cited instead.
</Finding>

<Finding label="FINDING 05" title="Not mentioned in authoritative sources">
Wikipedia entries, industry directories, press 
coverage, and third-party citations all increase 
AI confidence in a business. A business with zero 
press mentions and no third-party validation is 
invisible to AI regardless of how good the product is.
</Finding>
