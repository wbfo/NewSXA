A podiatrist in Cleveland had access to Semrush through her practice's marketing retainer. She could pull technical site audits, check keyword rankings, and review her backlink profile. She used it. She understood the reports. Her website technical health was solid.

She still appeared zero times in ChatGPT or Perplexity for "podiatrist Cleveland" or "foot doctor Cleveland." Her GBP was showing an outdated practice category from when she had handled a workers' comp caseload she no longer accepted. Her NAP was inconsistent across eleven directories, three of which were actively feeding her old address to Google's local algorithm. None of these issues appeared in Semrush. Semrush had not missed them through some failure — it had correctly reported what it was designed to check. The problem was that the most consequential issues for her new patient acquisition were outside Semrush's scope by design.

---

## What Semrush and Ahrefs Are Built to Check

Semrush and Ahrefs are industry-standard SEO platforms built to evaluate your website's performance in traditional Google organic search. Semrush's Pro plan crawls up to 100,000 URLs per project and audits approximately 140 technical issues: crawlability, indexation, page speed, broken links, redirect chains, duplicate content, missing meta tags, backlink profile, keyword ranking, and competitor comparison. Ahrefs covers approximately 100 technical checks with particular strength in backlink analysis and keyword research.

Both platforms have introduced limited AI visibility features in recent updates — Semrush now offers an AI Visibility Toolkit for tracking citation mentions across ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity. These are real improvements. They track whether your brand name is mentioned — they do not diagnose why you are absent, what schema changes would correct it, whether your NAP inconsistency is suppressing AI confidence, or what prioritized fixes would produce measurable improvement in your AI citation rate.

These are powerful, expensive tools built for a specific mandate. That mandate is organic search ranking. It is not full digital presence diagnosis.

<Finding label="FINDING 01" title="Semrush And Ahrefs Audit The Website; Sx Audits Audits The Full Presence">
Semrush and Ahrefs operate at the website layer. They evaluate the signals that determine how a website ranks in Google's traditional organic results. SX Audits evaluates the full digital presence across five dimensions: website technical health (which overlaps with what Semrush and Ahrefs check), schema markup and entity configuration, Google Business Profile status and accuracy, NAP consistency across 30+ directories and data aggregators, and AI platform visibility across ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews. The website technical layer is one of five dimensions. The dimensions that most commonly suppress small business lead acquisition — GBP, NAP, AI visibility, entity configuration — are outside the scope of any self-service SEO platform.

</Finding>

<Finding label="FINDING 02" title="No Self-service Platform Runs The AI Platform Test That Matters Most">
The most direct measure of AI visibility is a manual query run: opening ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews in fresh sessions and systematically running 15+ standardized queries — name, category and city, specialty, service type, comparison queries — recording whether the business appears, what the AI says about it, how accurate that description is, and which competitors are appearing in its place. Semrush's AI Visibility Toolkit tracks brand mention frequency across platforms. It does not run the diagnostic query protocol, assess the accuracy and sentiment of AI descriptions, identify which specific schema gaps are suppressing citations, or explain why competitors are appearing and the audited business is not. Data collection is not diagnosis. The manual AI query protocol produces findings that no automated tool can replicate.

</Finding>

<Finding label="FINDING 03" title="NAP Consistency Across 30+ Directories Requires Manual Audit">
The NAP consistency check that matters for AI confidence and Google Maps ranking requires verifying Name, Address, and Phone number across 30+ directories and data aggregators: Google, Yelp, Facebook, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Foursquare, Citysearch, Yellow Pages, Superpages, Manta, and the underlying data aggregators — Neustar Localeze, Infogroup, Acxiom — that seed dozens of smaller directories. Only 30% of small businesses have consistent NAP across major directories. Semrush and Ahrefs identify NAP as a concept in their local SEO guidance. Neither runs a 30+ directory check as part of their audit output. The podiatrist's eleven-directory inconsistency was not in any Semrush report. It was causing her Maps ranking to be suppressed, her AI citation confidence to be reduced, and prospective patients to encounter conflicting information. Manual directory audit is irreplaceable.

</Finding>

<Finding label="FINDING 04" title="Semrush And Ahrefs Provide Data; Sx Audits Provides A Prioritized Diagnosis">
This is the distinction that compounds across every dimension. Semrush returns a technical health score, a list of issues, and a category breakdown. It does not tell a podiatry practice in Cleveland that fixing their GBP category mismatch is worth doing before implementing structured data, and both are worth doing before addressing the backlink gap Semrush identified as a medium-priority issue. SX Audits delivers findings in priority order — the issues most likely to produce measurable improvement in lead acquisition if addressed first — with the specific action required for each. The data from a Semrush audit and the diagnosis from a professional audit are both useful. They are not interchangeable.

</Finding>

<Finding label="FINDING 05" title="The Tools Do Different Things And Serve Different Purposes">
This is not a competition between SX Audits and Semrush or Ahrefs. These tools were built for different purposes and are best used for those purposes. Semrush is the right tool for ongoing organic keyword tracking, backlink analysis, content gap identification, and competitor monitoring in traditional search. Ahrefs is strong for backlink research and keyword analysis. SX Audits is the right tool for full-spectrum digital presence diagnosis — specifically including the AI visibility, GBP, NAP, and entity dimensions that self-service platforms cannot evaluate. A business that uses Semrush monthly for SEO tracking and commissions an SX Audit annually for full-presence diagnosis is using both tools correctly.

</Finding>
