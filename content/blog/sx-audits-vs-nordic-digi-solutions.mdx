A small accounting firm in Tampere had just finished working with a web development agency. The new website was clean, fast, and professionally built. It loaded well on mobile. The design was credible and current. The agency had also set up a monthly SEO retainer to build organic traffic on the new site.

Three months later, the firm was not appearing in ChatGPT recommendations for "accountant Tampere" or "tax advisor Finland." Their GBP was still showing outdated service categories from before the website redesign. The new website had no schema markup despite the rebuild. Their NAP was inconsistent across seven Finnish directories because the new address format hadn't been propagated to any of them.

The web agency had built a strong asset. Nobody had checked whether that asset was configured to be found, cited, and recommended in the current discovery environment.

---

## What NordicDigi Offers

NordicDigi is a web design and development agency based in Turku, Finland. Their services include website development (from €1,450), Shopify store builds (from €1,850), Google Ads management (from €400/month), SEO services (from €450/month), social media management (from €650/month), and video editing (€270/minute). They describe themselves as the best web service company in Finland and focus on creating engaging user experiences and digital marketing execution.

NordicDigi builds and manages digital marketing channels. Their SEO offering addresses technical SEO excellence, Finnish keyword strategy, on-page content optimization, and backlink building. Their social media offering manages content across Facebook, Instagram, and YouTube. Their Google Ads offering handles campaign setup, keyword targeting, and performance management.

What NordicDigi does not provide: an AI visibility audit across ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google AI Overviews; a NAP consistency check across 30+ directories; a Google Business Profile status and category audit; schema markup configured for AI entity recognition; or a prioritized diagnosis of why a business is absent from AI-generated recommendations.

---

<Finding label="FINDING 01" title="Nordicdigi Builds Channels; Sx Audits Diagnoses Whether Those Channels Are Working">
Building a website, managing SEO, and running Google Ads are implementation services. They create and maintain digital marketing channels. A digital audit is a diagnostic service — it evaluates whether the infrastructure those channels are built on is configured correctly to be found, verified, and recommended across all discovery pathways. These are not competing services. They are different stages of the same work. The problem arises when implementation runs ahead of diagnosis: a well-built website on top of undiagnosed schema gaps, GBP errors, and NAP inconsistencies produces lower results than the investment warrants.

</Finding>

<Finding label="FINDING 02" title="SEO Retainers Do Not Include AI Visibility Auditing">
NordicDigi's SEO offering covers technical SEO excellence, Finnish keyword strategy, on-page optimization, and backlink building — the standard signals for Google organic ranking. Fewer than 12% of digital marketing agency teams have a defined strategy for Generative Engine Optimization. Only 12% of AI citations overlap with Google's top-10 organic results for the same query. A business that has invested in SEO through any agency — including NordicDigi — has invested in organic ranking, not in AI visibility. The accounting firm's SEO retainer was building organic ranking. It was not checking whether the firm was appearing in ChatGPT or Perplexity for the high-value, consideration-stage queries that AI platforms now handle.

</Finding>

<Finding label="FINDING 03" title="Finnish Market Context Makes The Gaps More Consequential, Not Less">
Finnish consumers are among the highest AI platform users per capita in Europe. The same shift in discovery behavior documented globally — from keyword search toward conversational AI recommendations — is occurring in Finland, affecting how prospective clients find professionals, local businesses, and service providers. A business that appears in ChatGPT recommendations for its Finnish city and category is positioned ahead of the majority of competitors who have optimized only for traditional Google search. NordicDigi's Finnish market SEO expertise addresses the traditional search layer. It does not address the AI recommendation layer that is increasingly determining new client discovery.

</Finding>

<Finding label="FINDING 04" title="A Fresh Website Build Is The Ideal Moment To Configure AI Readiness">
The accounting firm's website redesign was an opportunity. A new site is the most efficient moment to configure schema markup, structure content for AI citability, ensure NAP consistency, and verify GBP alignment with the new site architecture — because these configurations are easier to implement during a build than retroactively. Every week that passes without these configurations is a week the new asset is underperforming relative to its potential. An SX Audit conducted at the time of a new website launch — or shortly after — establishes the AI readiness baseline that makes the subsequent SEO and marketing investment work at full effectiveness.

</Finding>

<Finding label="FINDING 05" title="The Correct Sequence Is Diagnosis First, Implementation Second — For Any Agency's Work">
This applies regardless of which web or marketing agency a business works with. The implementation services NordicDigi provides — web development, SEO, Google Ads — are valuable. The diagnostic work SX Audits provides establishes the infrastructure baseline that makes those implementation investments work correctly. A business commissioning a new website from NordicDigi — or any agency — benefits from commissioning a digital audit alongside or shortly after the build, to ensure the AI readiness layer is configured before the marketing investment is made.

</Finding>
